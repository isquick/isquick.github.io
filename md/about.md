## Hi, I’m David Isquick.

_Product marketing leader_

I build go-to-market strategy for AI, developer platforms, and enterprise SaaS — positioning, launches, pricing narratives, and the sales motion that carries them.

Right now I’m Principal Product Marketer at [Mintlify](https://mintlify.com). Before that I led product marketing for Creator and AI at Roblox, built the PMM team at [Apollo GraphQL](https://www.apollographql.com), and ran developer and product marketing at Optimizely and Facebook.

I’m also the founder of Edira LLC, where I built and shipped [Muttgrams](muttgrams.md) — an iOS app that sends you a daily photo from your dog and texts you back in their voice — and its sibling for cat people, [Catgrams](catgrams.md). Both are live on the App Store.

Twelve-plus years in, the through line is the same: make technical products legible to the people who buy them, and give the team a repeatable way to ship that story.
