How I Work

		
The pattern underneath the launches above.

		
Start with the buyer, not the feature

		
Ideal customer profile first, then messaging. At Apollo that meant an ICP and ABM strategy built with
		marketing ops before a single sequence went out.

		
Make launches repeatable

		
Tiering models and lifecycle milestones turn launches from heroics into a process PM and PMM can run
		together, which is what the Mintlify framework is for.

		
Developers notice when you respect them

		
Freemium products, honest docs, and real technical content beat campaign volume. Rollouts grew 619%
		year over year on that premise.

		
Enable the people who carry the story

		
Pitch decks, sequences, and analyst briefings are where positioning either survives contact with the
		market or quietly dies.
