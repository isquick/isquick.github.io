Muttgrams

		
Founder · Edira LLC

		
Muttgrams is an iOS app that turns your dog into a text thread. Every day a photo
		“from” your pup lands in chat, and whatever you send gets a reply in their voice.

		

			Download on the App Store
			muttgrams.com
		

		
How it works

		
    
			
  • Add photos of your dog. Your daily Muttgram and the chat replies draw from the pictures you pick.
    • 
			
  • Pick their personality — goofy, chill, sassy, or a mix — so the messages sound like
			your dog instead of a generic bot.
    • 
			
  • Take care of them. Chatting earns treats, toys, and bones you can pass along to your pup.
    • 
		

		
Why I built it

		
Twelve years of marketing other people’s products left me wanting one of my own end to end,
		so I started Edira LLC and shipped Muttgrams to the App Store in July 2026: the product idea, the app,
		the AI chat, the subscription, and the marketing site. It’s a Tamagotchi crossed with a chatbot,
		and it is the shortest feedback loop I have ever had between a positioning decision and a real user.

		
Free on iPhone and iPad, with an optional Muttgrams Pro subscription.
