### How I work

The pattern underneath the launches.

#### Start with the buyer, not the feature

Ideal customer profile first, then messaging. At Apollo that meant an ICP and ABM strategy built with marketing ops before a single sequence went out.

#### Make launches repeatable

Tiering models and lifecycle milestones turn launches from heroics into a process PM and PMM can run together, which is what the Mintlify framework is for.

#### Developers notice when you respect them

Freemium products, honest docs, and real technical content beat campaign volume. Rollouts grew 619% year over year on that premise.

#### Enable the people who carry the story

Pitch decks, sequences, and analyst briefings are where positioning either survives contact with the market or quietly dies.

### Muttgrams

_Founder · Edira LLC_

Muttgrams is an iOS app that turns your dog into a text thread. Every day a photo “from” your pup lands in chat, and whatever you send gets a reply in their voice.

[Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/muttgrams/id6759312419) · [muttgrams.com](https://muttgrams.com)

#### How it works

- Add photos of your dog. Your daily Muttgram and the chat replies draw from the pictures you pick.
- Pick their personality — goofy, chill, sassy, or a mix — so the messages sound like your dog instead of a generic bot.
- Take care of them. Chatting earns treats, toys, and bones you can pass along to your pup.

#### Why I built it

Twelve years of marketing other people’s products left me wanting one of my own end to end, so I started Edira LLC and shipped Muttgrams to the App Store in July 2026: the product idea, the app, the AI chat, the subscription, and the marketing site. It’s a Tamagotchi crossed with a chatbot, and it is the shortest feedback loop I have ever had between a positioning decision and a real user.

Free on iPhone and iPad, with an optional Muttgrams Pro subscription.

Team cat? [Catgrams](md/catgrams.md) does the same thing for your cat.

### Catgrams

_Founder · Edira LLC · Live on the App Store_

Catgrams is Muttgrams for cat people: an iOS app that turns your cat into a text thread. Every day a photo “from” your cat lands in chat, and whatever you send gets a reply in their voice.

[Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/catgrams/id6797157394) · [catgrams.com](https://catgrams.com)

#### How it works

- Add photos of your cat and tell it their name, breed, and personality.
- Every day you get a photo “from” your cat, drawn from the pictures you pick.
- Text them, and they reply in their own voice. Chats are private to you — there’s no social feed.

Muttgrams proved the idea, so I shipped a second app for the other half of the pet world.
